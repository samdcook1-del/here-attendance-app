An attendance pass that lives on your iPhone, belongs only to you, and can't be lent to a friend. A concept for Regent's University London.

  • Soulbound passOne non-transferable token per student. It can't be sent, sold or borrowed.
  • Face ID on every check-inThe signing key sits in the iPhone's Secure Enclave and only unlocks for the pass holder.
  • Proof you're in the seatTap the seat tag, inside the campus geofence, inside the time window.
